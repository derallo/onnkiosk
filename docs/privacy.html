Privacy Policy — Onnkiosk

Effective Date: November 16, 2026 · Package: com.nernal.frame.oauth · Contact: derek.pangallo@gmail.com

Google API Services User Data Policy & Limited Use:
Onnkiosk's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Direct Device-to-Google Architecture

Onnkiosk runs exclusively on your Android tablet and communicates directly with Google APIs over HTTPS. We operate zero intermediate backend servers, zero analytics, and zero third-party data brokers.

2. Google Scopes & Data Usage

  • openid, email, profile: Displays your connected account name and email in Settings.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar: Displays your household schedules across Itinerary, 3-Day, Week, and Month views and event details.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/tasks: Displays your Google Task lists in Tasks and Shopping tabs and syncs checkbox completions or newly added reminders.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/photospicker.mediaitems.readonly: Displays user-selected photos from the Google Photos Picker when photo slideshow mode is enabled.

3. Local Storage, Retention & Deletion

OAuth tokens and offline dashboard caches are stored only in private local device storage (Android SharedPreferences and app cache). Tapping Sign Out inside the app immediately deletes all local tokens and cached data. You may also revoke access anytime in Google Account Permissions.
